Our process

Your path to a financial plan

  1. 01

    Assess your financial position

    We review income, expenses, assets and liabilities to see exactly where you stand today.

  2. 02

    Set your financial goals

    Define how much money you need, and by when, for each goal that matters to you.

  3. 03

    Assess your risk

    A short psychometric risk-assessment test shows how much market movement you can comfortably live with.

  4. 04

    Generate your financial plan

    You receive recommendations across mutual funds, NPS, PPF, FD/RD, insurance, loans and tax-saving schemes, including when and how much to invest.

  5. 05

    Execute your financial plan

    Execute on any platform you prefer. You can also use our own free platform for the recommended mutual fund investments, with no commission.

FAQ

Common questions

Who is this for?

Salaried professionals, business owners, families and corporates who want a structured plan instead of scattered investments. We have a lighter plan for simple needs and a full advisory service for larger portfolios.

How long does it take to get a plan?

After the questionnaire and risk assessment, our analysts prepare your plan and walk you through it. Timelines depend on how many accounts and goals are involved; we will confirm them in the first conversation.

Do I have to invest through you?

No. You can execute the plan through any platform. If you prefer, you can use our own free platform for recommended mutual fund investments, which carries no commission.

Will you keep reviewing my plan?

Yes. In the Monitor & adjust stage we track performance and update the plan when markets or your circumstances change.

What do I need to get started?

Basic details of your income, expenses, existing investments, loans and goals. Share a short brief and we will take it from there.
